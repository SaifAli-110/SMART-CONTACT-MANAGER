ABOUT OUR PROJECT

A Better Way to Manage Your Contacts

Smart Contact Manager is a web-based application designed to help users store, organize, search, and manage their personal and professional contacts effortlessly. With a clean interface and secure user authentication, managing your connections has never been easier.

👤

Easy Contact Management

Add, update, view, and delete your contacts in one convenient place. Keep names, phone numbers, emails, and other details organized.

🔍

Quick & Easy Access

Find and manage your saved contacts efficiently. Navigate through your contact list with a simple, user-friendly interface.

🔒

Secure User Account

Access your personal contact management space through user authentication and keep your contact information organized by account.

📄

Personalized Profiles

Manage your profile and maintain useful contact details, including profile images and descriptions, in one organized application.
