Help & Support

Welcome to Smart Contact Manager! Find helpful information to manage your contacts easily and securely.

Important Information

Keep your account information secure. Always log out after using your account on a shared device. Make sure your contact details are correct and up to date.

Manage Your Contacts

Add new contacts by entering their name, phone number, email address and other details. You can also update or delete contacts whenever necessary.

Search Contacts

Use the search feature to find a saved contact quickly. Enter a name or another supported search detail to locate the information you need.

Manage Your Profile

Keep your profile information updated. Check your account details regularly and use a strong password to protect your account.

Security & Privacy

Your contacts may contain personal information. Do not share your login credentials, and avoid accessing your account on untrusted devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I add a new contact?

Open the Add Contacts page, enter the required information, and submit the form to save the contact.

Can I edit or delete a contact?

Yes. Open your saved contacts and use the available edit or delete options, if provided.

What should I do if I cannot log in?

Check your email or username and password. If the problem continues, contact the application administrator.

How can I keep my account secure?

Use a strong password, never share your credentials, and log out when you finish using a shared device.

Still Need Help?

If you experience any issues while using Smart Contact Manager, please contact our support team or application administrator.
