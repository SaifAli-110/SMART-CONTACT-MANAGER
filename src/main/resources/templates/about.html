OUR PROJECT

Making Contact Management Simple & Efficient

Smart Contact Manager is a web-based application designed to help users store, organize, and manage their contact information from one convenient dashboard.

Instead of switching between notebooks, spreadsheets, or different applications, users can maintain their important connections in one organized place.

Our goal is to deliver a clean, accessible, and user-friendly experience that makes everyday contact management easier.
